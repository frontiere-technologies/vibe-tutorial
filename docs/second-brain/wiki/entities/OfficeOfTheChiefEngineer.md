# NASA Office of the Chief Engineer (OCE)

An office at [[NASA]] Headquarters. Source: [[nasa-systems-engineering-handbook-0-excerpt-trl]].

- Launched an initiative to improve the Agency's systems engineering infrastructure and capability, prompted by lessons-learned reports (NIAT, CAIB, Diaz Report and others); the Rev 2 update of the [[NASASystemsEngineeringHandbook]] is part of it.
- In 2014, together with the Office of the Chief Technologist, conducted an Agency-wide study on [[TechnologyReadinessLevel]] usage and Technology Readiness Assessment implementation; its guidance is going into a NASA TRA Handbook that will replace Appendix G (see [[TechnologyAssessment]]).
- [[StevenHirshorn]] is listed in the Office of the Chief Engineer.
