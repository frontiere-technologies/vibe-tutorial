# NASA Systems Engineering Handbook

NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev2, published by [[NASA]]. Source: [[nasa-systems-engineering-handbook-0-excerpt-trl]] (only an excerpt has been ingested).

- **History:** first published in 1995 (SP-6105); Rev 1 in December 2007; Rev 2 (2016) supersedes Rev 1. The update is part of an Agency-wide SE initiative of the [[OfficeOfTheChiefEngineer]].
- **Nature:** top-level guidance on good [[SystemsEngineering]] practice, "not intended in any way to be a directive"; companion to NPR 7123.1 and to Center-specific handbooks, and reference book for NASA SE training. It is an abridged version of the *NASA Expanded Guidance for SE*.
- **Method:** top-down compatibility with Agency policy plus bottom-up guidance from practitioners; communicates principles and alternatives rather than a single way of doing a task.
- **Structure:** six chapters — introduction, SE fundamentals, program/project life cycles, processes from concept to design, processes from design to product, crosscutting technical management — plus appendices. Appendix G covers [[TechnologyAssessment]] and [[TechnologyReadinessLevel]]s.
