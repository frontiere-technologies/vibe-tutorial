# NASA

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration, the US space agency. Source: [[nasa-systems-engineering-handbook-0-excerpt-trl]].

- Publishes the [[NASASystemsEngineeringHandbook]] (NASA/SP-6105) as top-level guidance on [[SystemsEngineering]].
- [[TechnologyReadinessLevel]]s were first described at NASA HQ in the 1980s.
- In 2014 the HQ [[OfficeOfTheChiefEngineer]] and the Office of the Chief Technologist ran an Agency-wide study on TRL usage and Technology Readiness Assessment (TRA) implementation; its findings are being collected into a NASA TRA Handbook (in work).
- Governing directives named in the source: NPR 7123.1 (*Systems Engineering Processes and Requirements*, including software TRL definitions) and NPR 7120.5 (source of the [[KeyDecisionPoint]] technology products).
