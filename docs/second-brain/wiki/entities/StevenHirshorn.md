# Steven R. Hirshorn

Chief Engineer of the Aeronautics Research Mission Directorate (ARMD), in the [[OfficeOfTheChiefEngineer]] at [[NASA]] Headquarters, at the time of the handbook. Source: [[nasa-systems-engineering-handbook-0-excerpt-trl]].

- Named as the contact for comments, questions and suggestions on the [[NASASystemsEngineeringHandbook]].
- Named in Appendix G as the HQ contact for questions on interpreting and applying [[TechnologyReadinessLevel]]s and Technology Readiness Assessments, pending the NASA TRA Handbook.
