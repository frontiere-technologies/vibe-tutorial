# Advancement Degree of Difficulty (AD²)

The second part of a [[TechnologyAssessment]]: after the [[TechnologyMaturityAssessment]] establishes current [[TechnologyReadinessLevel]]s, AD² determines what is required — and how difficult it is — to move a technology from one TRL to the next. Source: [[nasa-systems-engineering-handbook-0-excerpt-trl]] (§G.1, §G.3).

- Performed on all components, subsystems and systems that are below the requisite maturity level (Figure G.3-1).
- The initial AD² provides the material for preliminary cost and schedule plans and preliminary risk assessments; later assessments build the Technology Development Plan, alternative paths, fallback positions and performance descope options, and the milestones and metrics for Earned Value Management.
- A definition of AD² is one of the inputs required at the start of Pre-Phase A (§G.2).
