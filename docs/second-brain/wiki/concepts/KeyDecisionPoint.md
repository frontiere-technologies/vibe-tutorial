# Key Decision Point (KDP)

The gates at which a NASA program/project moves from one [[ProjectLifeCycle]] phase to the next. [[TechnologyAssessment]]s are conducted at various stages to provide the products each KDP requires. Source: [[nasa-systems-engineering-handbook-0-excerpt-trl]] (Table G.1-1, itself taken from NPR 7120.5).

| Gate | Transition | TA products required |
|---|---|---|
| KDP A | Pre-Phase A → Phase A | Assessment of potential technology needs versus current and planned [[TechnologyReadinessLevel]]s, and of opportunities to use commercial, academic and other government agency technology, as part of the draft integrated baseline. Technology Development Plan baselined: technologies to develop, [[HeritageSystems]] to modify, alternative paths, fallback positions and performance descopes, milestones, metrics and key decision points. Initial Technology Readiness Assessment (TRA) available. |
| KDP B | Phase A → Phase B | Technology Development Plan and TRA updated; incorporated in the preliminary project plan. |
| KDP C | Phase B → Phase C/D | A Technology Readiness Assessment Report (TRAR) demonstrating that all systems, subsystems and components have achieved a level of technological maturity with demonstrated evidence of qualification in a relevant environment. |

KDP C is where the TRL 6 threshold bites: "qualification in a relevant environment" matches the TRL 6 definition (prototype demonstration in a relevant environment). The final [[TechnologyMaturityAssessment]], basis of the TRAR, is performed just before the Preliminary Design Review.

The excerpt lists TA products only for KDP A, B and C.
